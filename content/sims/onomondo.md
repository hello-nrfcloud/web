This model comes with a **pre-activated** SIM card from onomondo which includes
10 MB free data. You may run out of data depending on your usage. If you
register the SIM card, you will get additional 40 MB to use within the SIMs
lifetime. In addition, you will get a two-month trial access to Onomondos
real-time network insight tool.

You can check the the amount of data left on your SIM card yourself by
registering for a free onomondo account.
